## Non-fast reference accessor

The x86-64 [syscall table](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/arch/x86/entry/syscalls/syscall_64.tbl#L198) assigns gettid186 and clock_gettime228. [CLOCK_MONOTONIC](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/include/uapi/linux/time.h#L50) is clock ID1. A direct `libc.syscall(228, 1, &timespec)` bypasses userspace/vDSO clock implementations. Return value, errno and normalized nanoseconds must be checked.

For the POSIX-timer implementation, [clock_gettime](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/time/posix-timers.c#L1136) selects the clock's get-timespec callback; clock_monotonic1452-1455 selects posix_get_monotonic_timespec217-221, which calls ktime_get_ts64 then adds the time-namespace monotonic offset. The non-POSIX [stub](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/time/posix-stubs.c#L39) also selects ktime_get_ts64 for monotonic.

The normal [ktime_get_ts64 reader](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/time/timekeeping.c#L955) uses tk_core.seq and retries if that sequence changes. It is distinct from helper5's fast view. It still shares the kernel timekeeper and underlying clocksource, so it is a separate accessor/reference path rather than an independent physical clock. The current host's read-only inventory shows zero monotonic time-namespace offset. Frozen execution must verify both collector and actor domains again; do not transfer an old namespace observation.

No simple high-resolution drop-in helper replacement is established. [Helper5](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/bpf/helpers.c#L177) uses mono_fast; boot helper125 and TAI208 use their fast variants too. Helper160 uses the coarse monotonic reader, which loses resolution and is not a replacement for precise phase analysis. Seeing ktime_get_ts64 as a BTF FUNC does not make it a permitted BPF kfunc. This review establishes no registered high-resolution non-fast kfunc for the raw-tracepoint program type, and does not claim an exhaustive kernel-wide absence proof.

## Minimal executable bounded clock control

A single held ordinary actor, authenticated by its live pidfd, namespace/start identity and typed-current task-storage cookie, can issue fixed controlled gettid186 calls. Before each gettid it obtains non-fast raw-syscall CLOCK_MONOTONIC value L; after it returns it obtains U. The scoped sys_enter BPF program emits only gettid records from that authenticated owner, with early/late helper5 values, emitter CPU observations and fixed sequence accounting. The unrelated raw clock228 calls do not become target gettid records. A gate-closed nonmember control and one-slot capacity-loss negative remain useful coverage controls.

The final fixed protocol uses 64 calls in eight alternating CPU0/CPU1 batches of eight, yielding 32 calls per CPU, with a 128-slot positive ledger. This establishes sample-specific numerical consistency and cross-CPU observations without an unrestricted trace. The final frozen protocol governs the execution order and result gates; the source review supplies accessor and boundary feasibility. Require all actual offered IDs, identities, expected CPU observations, exact attempt/retained/lost/fault counts, fixed ledger zero-padding, second detached ledger/hash, zero reported recursion misses and owned cleanup. Keep negative and positive source/raw/receipts. A verifier or attach failure is retained and ends the lane; no adaptive pressure or favorable retry. No installation or trace_clock/sysctl mutation is needed.

For each observed BPF point with numerical value t known by program ordering to occur between the reference reads, its compatible difference from reference-domain point time lies in `[t-U, t-L]`. Keep that full interval. If it contains zero, the observation is compatible with zero error; it does not prove zero error. If it excludes zero, the distance to zero is an observed minimum discrepancy under the reference assumptions. The larger endpoint magnitude is a conservative sample-specific envelope given those assumptions and the containing bracket. The bracket width includes observer/syscall/scheduling overhead and must be reported, without treating helper late-minus-early as certified callback duration.

Reject backwards raw reference brackets, malformed/unknown IDs or CPU observations, missing/duplicate rows, and counter loss/fault under the declared collection gates. A backwards helper5 observation, contradictory numerical early/late ordering, or helper timestamp outside its raw reference bracket is retained as a numerical outcome; it does not invalidate collection solely for that reason. The final frozen protocol governs this distinction. Do not intersect all samples to invent a constant clock offset: fast-view error may vary across updates/CPU. Finite controls do not prove a universal numeric bound, and no observed envelope transfers to the earlier Roslyn execution. Common-mode timekeeper/clocksource error remains outside the independent-accessor comparison.

## A source-feasible later post-context hook

The [scheduler header](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/include/trace/events/sched.h#L882) declares `sched_entry(bool preempt)` and `sched_exit(bool is_switch)`. [DECLARE_TRACE's BPF bridge](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/include/trace/bpf_probe.h#L120) gives these raw hook names `sched_entry_tp` and `sched_exit_tp` and builds raw_tp_map entries. Their absence from formatted available_events does not establish absence of a raw map.

The current pinned BTF has typedef IDs108674 and108678 for the two btf_trace hooks, both through pointer108675 to function-prototype108676 `(void *data, bool)`. The hidden data parameter is callback plumbing; raw argument0 is the one bool, not a task pointer. Live BTF hash exactly matches the previously pinned 1c1326be5d6ca0e60ad8790971b6765bba91cc8a3ab897a94794177484c8c9b9. BTF/prototype and source map presence establish source/host feasibility only; BPF_RAW_TRACEPOINT_OPEN must be a separately frozen gate, and none was attempted here.

Source [__schedule](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/sched/core.c#L6817) emits entry before scheduling, sched_switch at6941 before context_switch6944, then sched_exit_tp(is_switch) at6950 after the context_switch call returns. [context_switch](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/sched/core.c#L5348) calls switch_to and finish_task_switch; this is a software boundary bracket around register/stack transition, with substantial preparatory and cleanup work. It is not exact physical dispatch time.

Pairing entry and exit by same CPU is invalid. The outgoing task's scheduler invocation can suspend until that task later resumes, potentially on another CPU; the incoming task continues its own saved scheduling stack. The [finish_task_switch comment](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/sched/core.c#L5163) explicitly describes the restored stack/locals and possible CPU movement. Newly born tasks take [schedule_tail](https://github.com/microsoft/WSL2-Linux-Kernel/blob/d504d40ab83839cb61942a812a99d183c42227b9/kernel/sched/core.c#L5258), which emits exit(true) at5280 after finish_task_switch without that task having a matching earlier sched_entry. Exit(false) also exists for no-switch paths and cannot qualify a context switch.

A later controlled attachment protocol must therefore keep raw bool, emitter CPU, current authenticated generation, lifecycle/sequence and actor-control receipts. Typed-current pointer availability is not ownership; require the live cookie/private-generation checks before owner field reads or output. Dynamic children do not automatically inherit a preseeded task-storage cookie. Do not pair the next exit on a CPU to its preceding outgoing entry, assume all newborn exits have prior entries, or attach unscoped fentry as a shortcut. `finish_task_switch.isra.0` appears in available_filter_functions, while context_switch is always-inline and __schedule is notrace; these listings do not by themselves establish a safe BPF fentry attachment or ABI.

The presently frozen minimal sys_enter-only clock control intentionally leaves post-context-hook attachment, runtime dynamic pairing and physical scheduler duration unqualified. No clock-control result can alone certify GC pauses, exclusive GC/scheduler share or an Engine optimization.
